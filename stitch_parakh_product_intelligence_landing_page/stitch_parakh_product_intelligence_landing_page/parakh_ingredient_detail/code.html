Vitamin B3 • Dermatological Ingredient

Niacinamide

Also known as Vitamin B3 / Nicotinamide

Detected on product label Found in Verdant Botanicals Serum (Batch #VB-882)

What is it?

Niacinamide is a water-soluble form of Vitamin B3 commonly used in personal-care and cosmetic formulations. It is recognized for its versatility across different skin types and high formulation stability.

Molecular Family Pyridine-3-carboxamide
Functional Role Skin Conditioning Agent
Extraction Profile Synthetic & Biological Precursors

Why is it used?

Commonly used for skin conditioning, texture smoothing, and supporting moisture barrier balance.

1. Barrier Support

Helps preserve epidermal moisture and supports lipid synthesis.

2. Skin Conditioning

Maintains skin surface softness and balanced oil/sebum appearance.

3. Formulation Stability

Compatible with standard humectants (e.g. Glycerin, Hyaluronic Acid) without destabilizing pH.
